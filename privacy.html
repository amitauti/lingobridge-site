Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026 · Applies to LingoBridge for iPhone, version 1.0

In short: your voice and your learning progress stay on your iPhone. The only personal data LingoBridge can hold about you is an email address, optional name and account ID, and only if you choose to create an account. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking.

1. Voice and audio

  • When you practise, LingoBridge uses the microphone to listen while you speak. Recognition uses Apple's speech recogniser in its on-device mode, and tone analysis is our own code running on your phone.
  • Audio is processed in memory while you practise and is not saved to storage or sent to us or anyone else.
  • LingoBridge asks your permission for the microphone and for speech recognition. You can withdraw either in iOS Settings at any time.

2. Learning progress

  • Words learned, review schedules, attempt scores, streaks and settings are stored in a database on your iPhone.
  • This information is not uploaded to any server, including when you have an account.
  • Deleting the app removes it.

3. Optional account

  • You can use LingoBridge as a guest without giving us any personal information.
  • If you create an account, we collect your email address, a password and, if you enter one, your name, and an account user ID is created. These are handled by our authentication provider, Supabase, which stores them on our behalf. Passwords are stored as hashes by the provider; we never see them in readable form.
  • We use this information only to let you sign in and manage your account (app functionality). We do not use it for advertising or analytics, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties other than the provider that runs the sign-in service.
  • We keep it until you delete your account.

4. Deleting your account

In LingoBridge open Settings > Account > Delete account and confirm. Your account record (email, name, user ID and sign-in credentials) is permanently deleted from the authentication service straight away. If you cannot use the app, email amitauti.media@gmail.com and we will delete it for you.

5. Problem reports

The “Report a problem” feature saves a draft on your iPhone. Nothing is sent unless you press share and choose how to send it (for example Mail). A report contains only the dictionary, the word, the dictionary version, a category and your optional note.

6. Reminders

Optional daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. LingoBridge does not use remote push notifications.

7. Network use

LingoBridge connects to the internet only to sign in, sign out or delete an account if you use an account, and, in future releases, to download digitally signed dictionary updates that contain vocabulary data only and no personal information. Practice, speech recognition and tone checking work without a connection.

8. No tracking, advertising or analytics

  • No advertisements and no advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • No third-party analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

9. Children

LingoBridge is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: amitauti.media@gmail.com
