Speak, then get feedback

Hear a word, say it back, and see which syllables and tones landed. Choose Relaxed, Balanced or Strict checking.

Seven dictionaries

HSK 1 and 2, Everyday, Travel, Food, Business and Meetings. Switch the ones you want on or off.

Private by design

Speech recognition and tone analysis run on your device. Guest mode needs no account, and progress is stored only on your iPhone.

Devanagari or Pinyin

Pick the pronunciation guide that suits you: a Devanagari phonetic script with tone numbers, or Roman Pinyin.
