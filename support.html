LingoBridge Support

Help for the LingoBridge Mandarin vocabulary trainer for iPhone.

If you hit a problem, have a question or want to suggest a feature, email amitauti.media@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when it happened.

Reporting a wrong word, tone or translation

  1. Open the word (from a practice card or the Words tab) and tap Report a problem.
  2. Choose a category: Pinyin or tone, Translation or meaning, Audio or pronunciation, or Other.
  3. Optionally add a short note, then tap Save. The report is stored on your iPhone.
  4. Tap Send by email or share to send it through the iOS share sheet.
A report contains only the dictionary, the word, the dictionary version, the category and your note. It never includes your account, device details or learning history, and nothing is sent unless you choose to share it.

Troubleshooting

The microphone is not working

Open iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone and switch LingoBridge on. Do the same under Speech Recognition. In LingoBridge, Settings > Microphone & speech > Test my microphone checks that your input is picked up.

My speech is not recognised

LingoBridge uses Apple's on-device Mandarin (zh-CN) recogniser. Speak after tapping record, in a quiet place, at a normal pace. If it still misses a card you said correctly, use Mark as correct to move on, and try Settings > Practice > Pronunciation strictness > Relaxed.

I cannot hear the word

Check the ring/silent switch and volume. If you hear a robotic or missing voice, go to iOS Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices > Chinese and download a Mandarin voice.

Daily reminders

Reminders are optional local notifications. Turn them on under Settings > Practice and allow notifications for LingoBridge in iOS Settings when asked.

Guest mode, accounts and deleting your account

  • You can use LingoBridge fully as a guest, with no account. Progress is stored only on your iPhone.
  • An optional account (email and password) is used only to identify you. It does not back up or sync your progress.
  • To delete your account: open Settings > Account > Delete account and confirm. This permanently removes your account from our sign-in service. Your practice progress stays on your iPhone until you delete the app.
  • To sign out: Settings > Account > Sign out.
